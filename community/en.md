# Your first OKEY game

[English](en.md) | [简体中文](zh.md) | [Türkçe](tr.md) | [Deutsch](de.md)

For four-player learning sessions. Choose classic OKEY or 101 Okey before dealing. This starter uses the linked common rule descriptions; agree on local differences first.

## 1. Choose the game

| | Classic OKEY | 101 Okey |
|---|---|---|
| Playing set | 106 tiles | 106 tiles |
| Starting hands | Starter 15; others 14 | Starter 22; others 21 |
| Main task | Arrange a complete concealed hand | Open melds, then play out the rack |
| Standard opening | No 101-point opening | Own opening melds total at least 101 |
| Number 1 in a run | Commonly low or high: 1-2-3 / 12-13-1 | Low only in the cited rules: 1-2-3 |

The name “101” is not a tile count. Separate spare tiles from the playing set. For an automatic table, follow the model's loading instructions.

## 2. Recognize the joker

The **gösterge** is the exposed indicator. The next number of the same color is the **okey**; after 13 comes 1. Those two numbered tiles are wild. A **sahte okey** (false joker) represents that ordinary tile identity and is not freely wild.

Example: indicator Blue 8 → Blue 9 is wild → each false joker acts as an ordinary Blue 9.

## 3. Build legal groups

- Run: Red 3, Red 4, Red 5. Keep one color and consecutive numbers.
- Set: Red 7, Blue 7, Black 7. Use three or four different colors of the same number.
- Red 7, Red 7, Blue 7 is not a set. No tile belongs to two groups at once.

For classic OKEY, practice a 14-tile partition: Red 2-3-4-5 (4) + Blue 6-7-8-9 (4) + Black 10-11-12 (3) + Red/Blue/Yellow 1 (3). Assume the current joker identity is outside these tiles. This is a structure exercise; apply the table's finish and scoring rules.

For a standard 101 opening: Red 10-11-12-13 (46) + Blue 7-8-9 (24) + three colors of 11 (33) = **103**. Every group must also be legal. Pairs openings use a separate procedure.

## 4. Rehearse one turn

The starter uses the extra tile and discards first. Later turns take one permitted tile, make any legal play, then discard. Classic OKEY combinations normally stay concealed until the finish. In 101 Okey, taking a discard has conditions; in the cited rules it must be used immediately in a meld. Read the full guide before practicing exposed play, pairs or joker handling.

## 5. Agree before the first deal

Write down the version, dealing procedure, pairs rules, opening threshold, allowed discard claims, finish procedure, draw result and score direction. Use the printable agreement and score sheet linked below. Run one teaching hand before keeping scores.

## Check yourself

1. Indicator Black 13: which tile becomes wild, and what does a false joker represent?
2. Does a 101 opening totaling 98 qualify? Does 12-13-1 qualify in the cited 101 rules?

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. Black 1 becomes wild; a false joker acts as an ordinary Black 1.
2. No; no. A standard opening needs at least 101, and the cited 101 rules use 1 only at the low end.

</details>
