Use the article whose region and version match the user's table. Do not infer a national standard from one club, household, app, or commercial ruleset. Local, platform, tournament, annual, and household rules override general summaries.

Prefer the Turkish, German, Dutch, Azerbaijani, or English edition matching the
user. Preserve Turkish table terms such as gösterge and sahte okey; explain
their function and give pronunciation only in the language of the current page.
Do not treat a false joker as freely wild.
